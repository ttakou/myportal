1Signing in

The portal is a website. Open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari, on a computer or a phone:

https://addax-petroleum.mportals.com

The sign-in page shows the workspace name addax-petroleum under “Sign in to your account”. If you see a different workspace, check the address.

Sign in with Microsoft (staff)

  1. Click Sign in with Microsoft.
  2. Microsoft asks for your company email and password (the same ones as Outlook and Teams). Approve the sign-in on your phone if asked.
  3. You come back to the portal, on your Dashboard.

Your first sign-in

The first time you sign in, the portal looks for the staff record that already exists for you (your department, staff number, roles, offshore trips, crew and room). It matches you by your company email, or by your name when the roster has no email for you. When the match is clear you are linked straight away and see your own data.

If the match is not certain, your account waits for an administrator, who links you to the right record. Nothing is deleted in the meantime. Ask your administrator if you see a “pending” page.

Opening the link from Teams, Outlook or WhatsApp?Microsoft sign-in can fail inside these “in-app” browsers. The page then shows an amber warning. Tap the menu (⋯ or the share icon) and choose Open in browser, then sign in from Chrome or Safari.

The notification bell

  • The Offshore tab collects everything from the Offshore module, POB pages and manifests.
  • Notices that need a decision before a deadline are pinned on top, in an amber box called “Needs a decision”. The deadline is the night run, so the line reads for example “Decide before tonight's run (05:00 tomorrow)”. A notice leaves the pinned box once you have read it or the deadline has passed.

  • Emergency notices play a louder alarm and make the browser tab flash “🚨 EMERGENCY ALERT”.

4My trips & requests (every employee)

Open My Offshore → My trips & requests. From top to bottom the page has:

  • My offshore trips — your latest three trips with installation, dates, flight/room and status. Show all … trips lists the rest.
  • Request an offshore trip.
  • Offshore visitor requests — to bring visitors offshore.
  • Change my mob / demob dates — shown when you are on a rotation.

4.1 Request an offshore trip

  1. Pick the Installation (for example Juliet).
  2. Enter the Mobilise date and, if known, the Demob date.
  3. Write the Purpose of the trip (required), e.g. “SCADA upgrade”.
  4. Under People on this trip your name is already filled in. Add person adds another row: pick an employee from the list, or type a new name for a visitor or contractor who is not in the system yet.
  5. Click Request trip (N). Each person gets their own trip, so each can be cleared for HSE and put on a manifest separately.

4.2 Request a visit

  1. Click Request a visit.
  2. Choose the destination installation, the Departure date and the Return date. Leave Return empty for a day visit (back the same day).
  3. Tick Overnight (needs beds) if the visitors sleep offshore, and set the number of visitors.
  4. For each visitor give the full name, Company and Gender. The form warns you if a name already exists or looks similar — pick the existing person rather than creating a second one.
  5. Send the request.

4.3 Change my mob / demob dates

Use this when one hitch has to move. Only that hitch changes; your rotation stays the same.

4.4 How approval works

  • Date changes go first to your line manager (Endorse → OIM or Turn down, with a comment), then to the OIM, who approves or rejects. Statuses: Waiting for line manager → Waiting for OIM → Approved / Rejected.
  • A date change still undecided when the night run of its first day has gone expires (“Expired — not decided in time”). Send a new one if it is still needed.
  • Trip and visit requests are decided by the OIM. The Campboss then assigns a bed. They do not expire like date changes, but a trip that never happens is flagged 7 days after its mobilise date and cancelled after 30 days (you are told).
  • Approved date changes are carried out by the night run at 05:00 — you do not need to do anything else.

5The Today page and the night run

My Offshore → Today puts each role's work for the day on one page. The OIM, the Dispatcher and the Campboss each see their own box (admins see all three). Other staff see “Not available”.

5.1 The night run (05:00 Douala time)

Every night at 05:00 the system runs automatically. In order, it:

  1. carries out approved date changes;
  2. moves crews on and off by the rotation schedule (crew change runs automatically; only an administrator can switch it between Automatic and Manual);
  3. expires date changes nobody decided;
  4. sends reminders 3 days before a crew change;
  5. flags or cancels trips that never happened;
  6. prepares crew-change day manifests ahead and adds visitors to them, boarding and returning visitors on their dates;
  7. closes manifests whose day has passed;
  8. builds today's meal sheet;
  9. on Mondays, sends a 14-day look-ahead to the OIM and the Campboss.

People are only moved on their travel day — a manifest dated later never moves anyone early.

5.2 Morning reconciliation (Dispatcher)

The night run moves people as planned. After the boat or helicopter has gone, the Dispatcher records what really happened, on the run's card in Dispatcher — today's runs:

  • The banner says “Tonight's run moved X on and Y off — confirm who actually travelled”.
  • Tick box next to each passenger as they board (“N of M travelling ticked”).
  • No-show for someone who did not travel. This corrects POB: a MOB no-show comes off board, a DEMOB no-show stays aboard. No-shows fold into “Show N no-shows (not on the manifest)”; Put back on the run reverses a mistake.
  • Add traveller for someone who travelled but was not on the list. The search covers everyone in the people database and every registered visitor.
  • Confirm run when done: everyone not marked No-show is recorded as having travelled and the manifest closes (“Confirmed by … at HH:MM”).
  • Postpone to tomorrow if the whole run did not go (weather, breakdown): this morning's move is undone and everyone goes onto tomorrow's manifest. No-shows stay as they are.
  • Sign-off sheet opens the printable manifest (section 8).
RemindersIf a run is still not confirmed, the Dispatcher gets a reminder at 12:00 and the OIM at 15:00 (Douala time). Confirming or postponing the run clears them.

On the day of the screenshots there was no run, so the run card with tick boxes is not pictured.

9Trip Requests and Visitors

A trip request goes through three stages, shown as three counters at the top of Trip Requests:

  1. Awaiting the OIM — the OIM clicks Approve (or Approve (clear HSE)) or Reject.
  2. Awaiting a room — the Campboss clicks Assign a room and picks a room.
  3. Ready to travel — approved and bedded; the Dispatcher puts them on a manifest.

Visitors

Travel & Manifests → Visitors follows each visit from request to return: the OIM approves, the Campboss uses Find a bed, and on the day the visit is confirmed (Confirm offshore arrival, Confirm return onshore). Didn't travel closes a visit that did not happen and frees the bed.

POB & Live Board → Catering holds the meal sheet of the day: one line per person with Breakfast (05:00), Snack (09:00), Lunch (11:30), Dinner (17:30) and Lodging. 14Daily routines and common mistakes

OIM — every day

  • Open Today. Approve or reject everything in “decide before tonight's run” — undecided date changes expire.
  • Open Trip Requests via Decide → and decide new trip and visit requests.
  • Check the bell for pinned “Needs a decision” notices.
  • Glance at POB & Live Board → Overview: POB colour, overstayers, certification alerts.
  • After 15:00, if reminded, follow up any run the Dispatcher has not confirmed.
  • Mondays: read the 14-day look-ahead.

Dispatcher — every day

  • Open Today after the boat or helicopter has left. Tick who boarded.
  • Mark No-show for anyone who did not travel; Add traveller for anyone extra.
  • Confirm run — before 12:00 to avoid the reminder. If the run did not go at all, Postpone to tomorrow.
  • Print the Sign-off sheet when needed.
  • Check tomorrow's manifests in Travel & Manifests and the Look-ahead.

Campboss — every day

  • Open Today: “Arriving today without a bed” and “On board now with no room” should both be 0. If not, Open the bed board → and place them.
  • Assign rooms in Trip Requests → Approved, awaiting a room and to visitors (Find a bed).
  • Check the meal count and the Catering sheet; untick meals not served; print the meal sheet for the galley.
  • Keep the Free beds and Empty rooms tiles out of the red.

Common mistakes

SituationDo this
Someone was on the run but did not travelMark No-show on the Today page (or on the manifest). Never “demobilise” them to correct it — that creates a false trip in their history. No-show puts POB right and keeps them off the sign-off sheet.
Someone was mobilised by mistake todayUse Undo on the POB overview. It removes the trip as if it was never entered. It only works while the person is still on board and the mobilisation was made or dated today; after that, use Demob.
A visitor did not comeUse Didn't travel on the Visitors page. This closes the visit and frees the bed.
The same person appears twiceDo not create a third record or delete one. Ask an administrator to merge (link) the two records in the Admin console.
A date change was not decided in timeIt shows “Expired — not decided in time”. Send a new request if still needed.
Crews look out of step with the scheduleThe night run handles crew change automatically. Do not mobilise crews by hand unless an administrator has set Manual mode.
